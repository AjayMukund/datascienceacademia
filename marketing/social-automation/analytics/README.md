# Analytics Drop Folder

Drop your daily analytics exports here before 8:00 PM IST each day.

## Accepted Formats
- LinkedIn analytics CSV export
- Instagram insights screenshot (PNG/JPG)
- Instagram insights CSV (if exported via Meta Business Suite)

## File Naming Convention
Use today's date prefix so Routine 5 can find the right file:

    YYYY-MM-DD_linkedin.csv
    YYYY-MM-DD_instagram.csv
    YYYY-MM-DD_instagram.png

Example for May 14, 2026:
    2026-05-14_linkedin.csv
    2026-05-14_instagram.csv

## How to Export

### LinkedIn
1. Go to your LinkedIn Page
2. Analytics → Content → Export (top right)
3. Select date range: last 7 days or last 30 days
4. Download CSV → rename and drop here

### Instagram
1. Open Meta Business Suite → Insights
2. Export → CSV
3. Or take a screenshot of the summary dashboard
4. Rename and drop here

## What Routine 5 Analyzes
- Reach & impressions
- Saves (signals high-value content)
- Shares (signals viral potential)
- Comments
- CTR on link posts
- Follower growth
- Top performing post of the day
