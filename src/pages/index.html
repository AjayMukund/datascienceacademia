Questions

Frequently asked questions

Where is Data Science Academia located?
2nd Floor, Arasan Complex, No. 69, Grand Southern Trunk Road, West Tambaram, Chennai 600045 — a short walk from Tambaram railway station. Courses are delivered live in Chennai and online across India.
Do I need prior programming experience to join?
No. The Python Programming course and the AI Mastery Track are designed for absolute beginners — basic computer literacy and 10–12 hours a week are enough. Intermediate and advanced courses list their prerequisites on each course page.
Are DSA internships AICTE recognised?
Internships are aligned with NEP 2020 and AICTE guidelines. Where your institution is AICTE accredited the internship is AICTE aligned, and every intern receives a DSA Internship Certificate and Offer Letter. Internship details →
Which certifications can I prepare for at DSA?
Microsoft Azure AI-900, DP-900 and AZ-900, Power BI PL-300 and AWS CLF-C02 through our courses and the Microsoft Certification Program; UGC NET, GATE CS/IT, TNSET and PG-TRB through Competitive Examination Training.
Are sessions recorded, and is there placement support?
Yes — every live session is recorded for enrolled students. The AI Mastery Track adds resume and LinkedIn preparation, technical and HR mock interviews, and alumni-network job alerts.
What are the course fees?
Fees vary by course and programme. Call or WhatsApp {{ site.phone }} or email {{ site.email }} for current pricing and batch dates — we respond within one business day.
