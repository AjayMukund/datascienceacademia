Our story

Where Industry Meets Academia

Data Science Academia is a Ph.D.-led AI and data science institute in West Tambaram, Chennai. Since 2019 we have combined Anna University research depth with active AI engineering experience — so every concept we teach has been tested in production, not just in textbooks.

Est. {{ site.founded }} MSME registered AICTE-aligned Chennai · Online · Hybrid
Founded 2019 · Chennai

Built on one conviction

Data Science Academia was founded in 2019 with one conviction — that the best data science education cannot be detached from real engineering practice. What began as a division under Sai Chamundeeswari Academy (MSME Registered) is evolving into Data Science Academia Private Limited — incorporation in progress — bringing greater institutional credibility to every learner.

Our faculty brings together Ph.D.-level academic depth from Anna University with active AI engineering experience from industry, so every concept you learn has been tested in production, not just in textbooks.

Today DSA runs 18 specialised courses across five categories, seven programmes — from AICTE-aligned internships and Microsoft certification preparation to faculty development and research assistance — and DSA Code Champs, our school outreach wing for Grades 3–12. More than 500 students have trained with us, live in Chennai and online across India.

Our journey

From a single academy division to a Private Limited institute

  1. 2019

    Founded in Chennai

    Data Science Academia begins as a division under Sai Chamundeeswari Academy, teaching data science with a practitioner-first approach.

  2. MSME

    MSME registration

    Operating under Sai Chamundeeswari Academy's MSME registration — a formally recognised training provider for learners and institutions.

  3. AICTE · NEP 2020

    AICTE-aligned programmes

    Internships and Faculty Development Programmes aligned with AICTE guidelines and NEP 2020, recognised by institutions across Tamil Nadu.

  4. Grades 3–12

    Code Champs school wing

    DSA Code Champs brings AI and coding to school children across four levels — Scratch to computer vision — in schools across Chennai and online.

  5. In progress

    Private Limited incorporation

    Incorporation of Data Science Academia Private Limited is under way, bringing greater institutional credibility to every learner and partner.
