The DSA method

How projects work at DSA

Every project in this archive was completed inside a Data Science Academia internship: a real problem, a real dataset, a small team and a faculty guide who reviews the work from problem statement to final presentation.

🎓

Guided by faculty, not left to templates

All {{ n_projects }} projects were supervised by {{ guide }} — AI engineer, Ph.D. scholar at Anna University and founder of DSA. Students choose the problem; the guide sets the methodology and reviews every milestone.

👥

Small teams inside monthly cohorts

Projects run inside 1–2 month NEP- and AICTE-aligned internships that start every month. Teams of one to {{ tm.max }} students share a team leader, a mid-internship milestone presentation and a final review.

🔬

Real problems, real evaluation

Autonomous-driving aids in the CARLA simulator, Parkinson's freezing-of-gait detection, crop protection with CNNs, sarcasm detection, rainfall forecasting — compared across models with accuracy, precision and recall, not toy datasets.

📁

A portfolio you can show

Every intern leaves with a DSA Internship Certificate naming the project and mentor, a formal offer letter and a domain-specific project portfolio — evidence for placements, higher studies and research applications.
