How the two months work

Month 1 · December

Training

Live sessions every weekday evening take you from the machine learning workflow to regression, classification, tree ensembles, clustering and complete scikit-learn pipelines, with hands-on exercises on real datasets after every topic.

Month 2 · January

Group projects

You join a small group and build one end-to-end machine learning project, guided by the Internship Supervisor: problem statement, data, model, evaluation, a simple demo, and the final documentation and presentation.

The 8-week plan

  1. Month 1 — Training
  2. Week 1
    Python and data for machine learningNumPy and pandas refresher, exploratory data analysis, the machine learning workflow, train/validation/test splits.
  3. Week 2
    Regression and classificationLinear and logistic regression, regularisation, and the metrics that matter: MAE, RMSE, R², precision, recall, F1.
  4. Week 3
    Trees and ensemblesDecision trees, random forests, gradient boosting and XGBoost, and reading feature importance.
  5. Week 4
    Unsupervised learning and pipelinesK-Means and PCA, cross-validation and tuning, handling imbalanced data, and building a full scikit-learn pipeline.
  6. Month 2 — Group projects
  7. Week 5
    Teams and problem statementsGroups formed, project topics assigned, datasets collected, project plan agreed with the Internship Supervisor.
  8. Week 6
    Data and baselineCleaning, exploratory analysis, feature engineering and a first baseline model.
  9. Week 7
    Improve and reviewBetter models, tuning and error analysis; mid-project review with the supervisor.
  10. Week 8
    Demo, documentation and presentationA simple web demo of the model, the project documentation, and the final presentation and evaluation.

The Internship Supervisor may adjust the pace of the plan to suit the batch.

Example project topics

  • Student performance prediction from academic and attendance data
  • Loan default risk scoring
  • Customer churn prediction for a subscription business
  • House price estimation for Chennai localities
  • Crop recommendation from soil and weather data
  • Fake job posting detection

Final topics are assigned to each group by the Internship Supervisor.
