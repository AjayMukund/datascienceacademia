The DSA method

How every course works

Whichever course you choose, the format is the same: live teaching by a practitioner, real data in every session, and a clear route from the classroom to an internship or a job.

🎥

Live sessions, recorded for replay

Every session is taught live — at our Chennai centre or online — and the recording is available to enrolled students to replay at their own pace.

📊

Hands-on projects on real datasets

Each course ends in a capstone built on real-world data — an end-to-end EDA pipeline, an analytics report on an e-commerce dataset, a deployed model or a production LLM application.

🎓

Ph.D.-led, practitioner-taught

Your instructor is a practising AI engineer and Ph.D. scholar at Anna University, so every concept is taught the way it is actually used in production.

📜

Certificate on completion

Complete the course and its capstone to receive a Data Science Academia certificate. Employers and institutions can verify a certificate online.

🧭

Internship pathways

Move from a course into a 1–2 month NEP- and AICTE-aligned internship with a formal offer letter, real project work and a DSA Internship Certificate. Cohorts start monthly.

🤝

Mentoring and career support

Internship projects run under Ph.D.-qualified mentors, and the AI Mastery Track adds weekly one-on-one check-ins, mock interviews and placement assistance.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What are the course fees?
Fees vary by course and programme. Call or WhatsApp {{ site.phone }} or email {{ site.email }} for current pricing and batch details — we respond within one business day.
Are the courses online, offline or hybrid?
All three. Live online sessions are recorded for replay; offline workshops and bootcamps run at our centre in West Tambaram, Chennai; hybrid combines online sessions with offline workshops. Tell us your preference when you enquire.
Do I receive a certificate?
Yes. Complete the course and its capstone project to receive a Data Science Academia certificate of completion. Internships add a DSA Internship Certificate and offer letter, and the Microsoft Certification Program prepares you for AI-900, DP-900, PL-300 and AZ-900.
What are the prerequisites?
Python, SQL, R and Excel need no programming experience — basic computer literacy is enough. Tableau and Power BI need no programming either. Intermediate courses such as Machine Learning, NLP and Data Visualization assume Python; advanced courses such as Deep Learning, Computer Vision, LLMs and Generative AI assume Machine Learning. Exact prerequisites are listed on every course page.
When does the next batch start?
Batch dates depend on the course and delivery mode; internship cohorts start every month. Contact us for the current schedule — we reply within one business day.
