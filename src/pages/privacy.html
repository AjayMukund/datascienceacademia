Services that process data for us

  • Formspree delivers enquiry-form submissions to our inbox.
  • Zoho Mail hosts our email.
  • Google Forms, Google Sheets and Google Drive collect and store internship applications, including payment screenshots, in our Google account. Only we can see them. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Microsoft Clarity records anonymised visits (heatmaps and session replays) so we can improve the site. It masks everything typed into forms, so your name, email and phone number are never captured. Microsoft's privacy statement: privacy.microsoft.com.
  • Google Analytics counts visits and tells us which pages lead to enquiries (for example, that someone sent the form or tapped the WhatsApp button, but not what they wrote). Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Chatbase runs the chat assistant. It loads only after you interact with the page. Please don't share sensitive personal details in the chat.
  • Supabase hosts student-portal accounts and learning records.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website, and jsDelivr serves some code for the student portal. Like any web host, they see your IP address when they deliver a page.

Some of these providers store data outside India. Each is bound by its own privacy and security commitments.

Cookies and storage on your device

  • Theme preference: if you switch between light and dark mode, your choice is saved in your browser.
  • Offline cache: the site saves some of its own files on your device so pages load faster on later visits.
  • Analytics cookies: Google Analytics (_ga) and Microsoft Clarity (_clck, _clsk) set cookies to recognise repeat visits. You can block or delete them in your browser settings, and the site will still work.
  • Portal sign-in: signing in to the student portal stores a session token so you stay signed in.

Children

Code Champs is for students aged 8 to 17. Enrolment details for a child should come from a parent or guardian, and we use them only to run the programme. If you think a child has sent us information without a parent's consent, contact us and we'll delete it.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as needed to answer and follow up, and student records for as long as your account is active. We keep internship applications and payment records for the batch and for our accounts, and delete payment screenshots once they are no longer needed for that purpose. We keep certificate records so that certificates can still be verified later. When information is no longer needed, we delete it.
