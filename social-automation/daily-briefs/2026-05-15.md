  - *Instagram:* Short-form Reel version targeting final-year engineering students who need this wake-up call before entering the wrong career lane.

- **Format:** LinkedIn Document Carousel (PDF, 8–10 slides). Slide structure:
  - Slide 1: Hook — "1,002 Indian tech jobs lost per day in 2026"
  - Slide 2: Which jobs? (IT services roles being cut, with real company names)
  - Slide 3: Why? AI doing what they did — not science fiction, specific tasks
  - Slide 4: Which roles are being created? (Azure Data Engineer, AI Engineer, ML Ops)
