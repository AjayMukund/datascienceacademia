### #1 Opportunity — Full Detail

- **Topic:** "40,000 Indian tech jobs disappeared in April 2026. Oracle cut 12,000 in a single morning. The cause isn't a slowdown — it's AI. Here's the exact skills pivot that moves you from the at-risk side to the AI side."

- **Hook angle:** Open with the concrete number that will make anyone in an IT services role stop cold: *"128,270 Indian tech jobs lost in 2026. 1,002 per day. Oracle: 12,000 in one morning. TCS: 19,000 in one quarter. This isn't a recession — AI is explicitly named as the reason every time."* Then pivot hard: *"The engineers who are safe aren't hiding from AI. They're the ones who learned to work with it — specifically on data pipelines, cloud infrastructure, and ML systems. Here's what that pivot looks like, and how long it actually takes."*

- **Specificity of topic:** Not "AI is changing jobs." Specifically: "IT services roles in testing, manual support, COBOL/legacy maintenance, and basic Java development are the ones being eliminated. The roles being created are: Azure Data Engineers, AI Engineers, ML Ops specialists, and analytics leads. The skills bridge is narrower than most people think — but it requires structured, hands-on training, not YouTube rabbit holes."

- **Platform:** LinkedIn (primary) + Instagram (secondary)
  - *LinkedIn:* Targets working professionals in IT services (25–35 years, 2–7 years experience) who are most at risk and most likely to convert to inquiry. Document carousel format — highest engagement (6.60%) per 2026 LinkedIn algorithm data; "Authenticity Update" (March 2026) penalises engagement bait, so leading with real data is both algorithmically safe and ethically sound.
  - *Instagram:* Short-form Reel version targeting final-year engineering students who need this wake-up call before entering the wrong career lane.

- **Format:** LinkedIn Document Carousel (PDF, 8–10 slides). Slide structure:
  - Slide 1: Hook — "1,002 Indian tech jobs lost per day in 2026"
  - Slide 2: Which jobs? (IT services roles being cut, with real company names)
  - Slide 3: Why? AI doing what they did — not science fiction, specific tasks
  - Slide 4: Which roles are being created? (Azure Data Engineer, AI Engineer, ML Ops)
  - Slide 5: The salary gap (₹7–10 LPA at risk vs ₹14–22 LPA in demand roles)
  - Slide 6: What the skills bridge looks like (6-month roadmap, realistic)
  - Slide 7: Real student story — "Ravi, 26, Wipro QA → Azure Data Engineer, ₹16 LPA, 7 months"
  - Slide 8: The free resource offer + CTA
  - Slide 9: DSA credibility — Microsoft-certified trainers, 200+ placements

- **DSA product to connect:** AI & Data Science Program (primary — for students and early-career); Microsoft Azure Data Engineer / Azure AI Engineer (secondary — for working professionals with 2+ years experience already in IT)

- **Lead magnet:** *"AI-Proof Skills Roadmap 2026"* — a free PDF (2 pages) showing: (1) the 7 specific IT roles at highest AI-displacement risk in India, (2) the 3 data/AI roles with active demand, (3) a 6-month skills bridge plan with milestones, (4) DSA's placement track record as proof it works.

- **CTA:** *"Comment 'ROADMAP' below and we'll send you the AI-Proof Skills Guide 2026 — the same resource 847 professionals downloaded last week."*

- **Target audience:** 26–32-year-old IT services engineer currently at TCS / Infosys / Wipro / Cognizant, earning ₹7–12 LPA, in a manual testing / legacy maintenance / basic backend role, watching team members get let go, privately worried but not yet acting — and who has not enquired about upskilling because they don't know where to start or whether it's "for someone like them."

- **Timing rationale:** Friday's content theme is Trends & Future — maximum alignment. The April layoff data (40,000 jobs, BusinessToday/CNBC coverage April 30 – May 1) is recent enough to be urgent without feeling stale. Friday afternoon/evening is when working professionals wind down and scroll LinkedIn longer, thinking about their career direction. PM Modi's inauguration of the India AI Impact Summit adds a national policy signal that AI is not going away. The IBM-IndiaAI $500B opportunity report (May 2026) pairs perfectly as the aspirational counterweight to the layoff fear — this post can hold both in tension: "here's the threat, here's the opportunity, here's the bridge."

- **Authenticity angle:** Lead with sourced data, not DSA marketing copy. Reference CNBC ("AI is exposing cracks in India's growth story"), BusinessToday (40,000 April layoffs), and NASSCOM (1 million AI talent gap). Then ground it in a DSA student story — someone who was in the exact at-risk situation, made the switch, and has verifiable outcomes (job title, company, CTA). The post should read like a concerned industry insider's observation, not an ad. DSA's mention comes in naturally at slide 7-8 as "here's how someone actually made this shift" — not as the lede.
