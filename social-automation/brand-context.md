## Target Audiences
- **Students:** Engineering, BSc, BCA — want jobs and internships
- **Professionals:** Looking to upskill, switch careers, or get certified
- **Researchers:** Need AI tools and research methodology support
- **Institutions:** Want corporate training or curriculum partnerships
- **Corporate clients:** Need workforce upskilling in AI/Data Science

## Primary Platforms
- **LinkedIn** — authority, lead generation, professional audience
- **Instagram** — awareness, engagement, younger students

## Brand Voice Rules
- Open with a strong hook — never a generic statement
- Use numbers and specifics ("3 skills", "₹12 LPA", "72% of AI jobs")
- Educational first, promotional second
- Always end with a clear CTA tied to a lead magnet or conversion
- Avoid jargon dumps — explain concepts simply
- Use relatable pain points: "still using Excel?", "rejected in interviews?"
